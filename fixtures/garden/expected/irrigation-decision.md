# Irrigation Decision Framework

Community garden irrigation decisions balance water allocation rates, equipment
category selection, and maintenance scheduling.

## Water Allocation Context

The district base rate is 4.5 units per plot per growing season, as confirmed
by the revised rate schedule. Earlier documents referenced 4 units; the revised
schedule supersedes those references.

## Equipment Selection Guidance

For most plots, Category A (standard emitter) or Category B (pressure-compensating)
systems provide adequate performance. Category C (subsurface drip) offers the
lowest ongoing water consumption at higher upfront cost.

## Maintenance Schedule

Annual connector inspection before each growing season is recommended for all
system categories. Budget two replacement emitters per plot per season as
preventive maintenance.

## Infrastructure Considerations

Aging infrastructure in high-use sections warrants phased replacement planning.
Replacement cost estimates should be validated against current district schedules.
