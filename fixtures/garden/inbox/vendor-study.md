# Vendor Equipment Study: Drip Irrigation Comparison

Prepared by the Garden Supply Cooperative Research Committee.

This study compares three categories of drip irrigation equipment available
to community garden operators in temperate climates.

## Category A: Standard Emitter Systems

- Cost range: 5 to 8 units per plot
- Water use rate: 3.2 units per season at standard pressure
- Maintenance: Annual connector inspection recommended

## Category B: Pressure-Compensating Systems

- Cost range: 12 to 18 units per plot
- Water use rate: 3.2 units per season regardless of pressure variation
- Recommended for plots with significant elevation change

## Category C: Subsurface Drip Systems

- Cost range: 20 to 30 units per plot
- Water use rate: 2.8 units per season due to reduced evaporation
- Higher upfront cost, lower ongoing water consumption

## Water Rate Discrepancy Note

The cooperative's data shows the district base rate may be 4.5 units per season
per plot, not 4 units as stated in some district documents. The cooperative
recommends operators verify the current tariff schedule directly.

## Conclusion

For most community garden applications, Category A or B systems offer the best
balance of cost and reliability.
