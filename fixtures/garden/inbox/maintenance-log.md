# Maintenance Log: Riverside Community Garden Irrigation

Season maintenance log maintained by the volunteer facilities committee.

## Spring Inspection Notes

- Plot 3: Emitter clogged, replaced with spare from stock.
- Plot 7: Connector showing UV degradation, flagged for replacement.
- Plot 11: No issues found.
- Eastern main access hatch: Hinge needs lubrication.

## Summer Mid-Season Check

All systems operating within normal parameters.
Water pressure readings consistent across all zones.
No leaks detected at the main distribution points.

## Notes for Next Season

The committee recommends budgeting for two replacement emitters per plot
per season as a preventive measure. Current stock level is adequate for
one additional season without new purchases.

Drip irrigation systems have reduced visible runoff compared to the
previous overhead spray system. Plot holders report satisfaction with
water distribution.
