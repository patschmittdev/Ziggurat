# Municipal Water Infrastructure Report

The Millbrook Municipal Water District conducted its annual review of irrigation
infrastructure in the Riverside Community Garden District.

Key findings:

- Twelve community garden plots currently use drip irrigation systems.
- The district supplies water at a base rate of 4 units per growing season per plot.
- Aging infrastructure in the eastern section shows signs of wear.
- A full replacement of the eastern main would cost approximately 200 resource units.

The board recommended a phased maintenance schedule beginning in the spring.

Water usage for the current season was within the expected parameters for the district.
No significant overages were recorded. The district's reservoir levels remain stable.

Recommendations:

1. Inspect all drip-line connectors before the next growing season.
2. Consider a co-operative purchasing arrangement for replacement parts.
3. Review the per-plot allocation formula to account for plot-size variation.
