# Revised Water Rate Schedule

Effective notice from the Millbrook Municipal Water District.

The district has revised its allocation schedule for community garden users.

## Updated Allocation Rates

The base allocation per plot per growing season is now 4.5 units.
This replaces the prior rate of 4 units referenced in earlier district documents.

The change reflects updated infrastructure costs and reservoir management targets.

## Effective Date

The revised rate applies beginning with the next full growing season.
Existing pre-paid allocations at the prior rate will be honored for the
current season only.

## Appeals Process

Plot operators who believe their allocation is insufficient may submit a
variance request to the district water board before the end of the current
administrative period.

Contact the district office for the current variance request form.
