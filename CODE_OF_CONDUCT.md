# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Ziggurat welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and
experience levels. Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and disagreements with patience and technical specificity
- critique work rather than people
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience
- accept correction and help keep discussions focused
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and deliberate
  disruption

Security research performed in good faith and reported through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) is welcome.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement
decisions should protect affected participants and avoid publishing private details.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through GitHub. For sensitive conduct
reports, ask a maintainer to arrange a private channel before sharing details.
For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), which records
current private reporting availability.
